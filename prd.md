## 1. Product Summary

Infintin is a motor and controller design company shifting its primary market focus from the electric-vehicle industry to HVAC components. The redesigned website should present Infintin as an engineering partner for HVAC OEMs, suppliers, traders, and contractors, initially emphasizing fan coil unit (FCU) motors and controller solutions.

The website is a B2B product portfolio and lead-generation channel. It must help engineering and procurement teams understand the product, the problem it addresses, Infintin's engineering capabilities, and how to initiate a technical discussion or paid proof of concept (PoC)/pilot. It should also give investors and ecosystem partners a clear, credible view of the company's product-market focus and development progress.

### Product at a glance

- **Company:** Infintin Mobility Solutions
- **New primary market:** HVAC components
- **Initial application focus:** Fan coil unit motors, with air handling unit (AHU) motors also identified as a target area
- **Known product:** FCU Double Shaft Motor
- **Product stage:** Prototype, currently under laboratory testing according to the supplied company information
- **Primary conversion:** Qualified product enquiry or paid PoC/pilot discussion
- **Secondary outcomes:** Demonstrate engineering capability, attract OEM customers, attract investors, and create partnership opportunities

## 2. Business Context and Current-State Problem

The current website positions Infintin mainly around rare-earth-free electric motors for clean mobility and lists legacy traction-motor products. The company has since shifted its commercial focus toward HVAC motor and controller solutions. The redesign must make this shift obvious within the first screen and consistently across navigation, products, project descriptions, metadata, and calls to action.

Current website for reference: https://www.infintin.in/

### Redesign problems to solve

1. **Positioning mismatch:** The existing EV/traction-led positioning does not reflect the new HVAC priority.
2. **Product clarity:** The FCU Double Shaft Motor needs a clear product page with confirmed specifications, application fit, features, status, and an enquiry path.
3. **Credibility gap:** The prototype is still being tested, so the website needs to demonstrate engineering process and progress without implying commercial validation that has not happened yet.
4. **Lead-generation gap:** Prospective OEMs need a straightforward way to request a technical discussion, sample evaluation, PoC, or pilot.
5. **Evidence gap:** Energy savings, payback, noise, efficiency, and rare-earth claims must be accurately qualified and backed by evidence before being presented as proven results.

## 4. Target Audiences

### Primary: HVAC OEM engineering and procurement teams

**What they need:** A motor/controller supplier or development partner that understands FCU requirements, has a credible product-development process, and can discuss integration and evaluation.

**Likely questions:**

- Is the motor designed for my FCU configuration and capacity?
- What are its power, speed, voltage, dimensions, shaft, mounting, noise, and control specifications?
- What performance has actually been measured?
- What changes or integration work would be required?
- Can Infintin support a PoC or pilot, and what is the current development stage?

**Desired action:** Request a technical discussion or paid PoC/pilot.

### Secondary: HVAC suppliers, traders, contractors, and channel partners

**What they need:** A concise overview of the product's use case, availability, lead-time expectations, support model, and the process for introducing it to customers.

**Desired action:** Submit a product or partnership enquiry.

### Secondary: Investors, incubators, and ecosystem partners

**What they need:** A trustworthy view of the problem, product focus, development status, technical differentiation, recognition, and next milestones.

**Desired action:** Contact Infintin to discuss the company or partnership opportunities.

## 5. Positioning and Messaging

### Proposed positioning statement

> Infintin designs motor and controller solutions for HVAC applications, beginning with fan coil unit motors. Its engineering approach combines application-specific design with locally sourced materials and local manufacturing partners to reduce dependence on imported components and improve supply-chain responsiveness.

This is a draft positioning statement, not approved website copy. Confirm the sourcing and manufacturing wording before publishing it.

### Core message hierarchy

1. **Primary:** HVAC-focused motor and controller engineering.
2. **Product:** FCU Double Shaft Motor designed for fan coil units in the stated 1 to 2 TR range, subject to confirming the final application envelope.
3. **Engineering value:** Motor and controller development tuned for HVAC operating requirements.
4. **Supply-chain value:** Local design and sourcing approach intended to reduce import dependency and improve lead-time responsiveness.
5. **Commercial next step:** Discuss a technical evaluation, paid PoC, or pilot with the engineering team.
6. **Credibility:** Prototype status, development process, awards, and support ecosystem, presented with clear labels and permission to display any third-party marks.

### Suggested brand attributes

- Engineering-led
- Specific and evidence-based
- Practical and commercially aware
- Transparent about development status
- Modern industrial B2B, rather than consumer EV branding

### Messaging guardrails

- Do not imply the FCU motor is commercially available or production-ready while its status is prototype/lab testing.
- Do not present projected energy savings or payback as independently verified field results without supporting test data.
- Clarify the exact meaning of **rare-earth-free** versus **heavy-rare-earth-free**. These terms are not interchangeable. The supplied notes use both “HRE/RE free” and “non-rare-earth-based magnets”; technical confirmation is mandatory before publication.
- Avoid “100% made in India,” “fully indigenous,” “zero maintenance,” or similar absolute claims unless specifically substantiated and approved.
- Do not expose proprietary algorithms, methods, software, or detailed IP-sensitive design information.
- Do not imply that all manufacturing is performed in-house. The supplied process says commercial production and prototype development involve external parties/partners.
- If a performance figure is preliminary, label it as a target, estimate, simulated value, or lab result as appropriate. Never label a target as a measured result.

## 10. Functional and Non-Functional Requirements

### Functional requirements

- Responsive navigation and layouts across common mobile, tablet, and desktop sizes
- Product enquiry/contact form with validation and success/error states
- Clear CTAs from the home page, product page, project page, and footer
- Reusable content structure for future HVAC products and projects
- Download links only for approved public technical documents
- Functional links to approved company social profiles and email/phone details

### Accessibility

- Semantic HTML and a logical heading hierarchy
- Keyboard-accessible navigation, forms, controls, and focus states
- Labels and helpful validation messages for all inputs
- Sufficient color contrast and visible focus indicators
- Meaningful alt text for product and engineering images
- Do not use color as the only way to communicate prototype status or form errors

### Performance and reliability

- Optimize product/lab images and use responsive image sizing
- Avoid heavy animation that obscures technical information or delays first content
- Minimize unnecessary client-side JavaScript
- Ensure forms fail gracefully and communicate submission outcomes
- Test on current mainstream desktop and mobile browsers

### SEO

- Use page titles and meta descriptions aligned to HVAC motors, FCU motors, motor controllers, and custom engineering, without keyword stuffing
- Use one clear H1 per page and meaningful heading hierarchy
- Add canonical URLs and sitemap/robots configuration as appropriate to the chosen platform
- Use descriptive image filenames and alt text
- Add structured data only where accurate and appropriate
- Avoid indexing retired legacy product pages as though they are active products; use redirects or archive handling where appropriate

### Integrations

No CRM, WhatsApp, maps, or external API integrations were requested. The contact form still requires a confirmed submission method and notification destination. Analytics and form spam protection should be treated as implementation choices requiring approval, not pre-approved integrations.

## 11. Content and Visual Direction

### Visual principles

- Industrial, precise, credible, and product-led
- Show real motors, controllers, test setups, engineering work, and the team where available and approved
- Prioritize technical clarity over decorative visuals
- Use diagrams, dimensions, specifications, and test charts only when the source data is verified
- Use clear status labels such as “Prototype” and “Under laboratory testing” where relevant
- Make the primary CTA visually prominent without making the site feel like a consumer app or an aggressive sales landing page

These sites are references for patterns and presentation, not copy or design assets to duplicate.

## 13. Acceptance Criteria

The initial release is acceptable when:

- The HVAC focus is clear from the first screen and reflected in page metadata and main navigation.
- The FCU Double Shaft Motor is the primary featured product.
- The product's prototype status is prominent and accurate.
- No legacy EV product is represented as an active HVAC offering, and any retained EV content has an explicit approved purpose/status.
- All published product specifications are confirmed by the company.
- Energy savings, payback, rare-earth, noise, efficiency, local manufacturing, and lead-time statements are either evidence-backed and qualified or omitted.
- The development project is clearly presented as an in-progress prototype, not a completed customer deployment.
- A visitor can initiate a technical enquiry or paid PoC/pilot discussion from key pages.
- The enquiry form is accessible, validated, and has reliable success/error states.
- Company names, award claims, team details, partner/support wording, and logos are verified and approved for public use.
- Core pages work on mobile and desktop, use semantic structure, and meet agreed performance/accessibility checks.
- No proprietary methods or confidential technical details are exposed.
